// 06 FAQ

Common questions

Does rooting void my warranty? +
It often can, depending on the manufacturer and region. Check your device's warranty terms before unlocking the bootloader.
Will unlocking the bootloader wipe my data? +
On most devices, yes. Unlocking erases the storage. Back up everything first.
What is Magisk? +
Magisk is a widely used tool for systemless root and modules. Only install modules you trust, and read what each one changes.
What if my phone bootloops? +
Stay calm and don't flash random files. Check the recovery section of the guides, and ask in the Telegram community with your model and the steps you took.
Is this site affiliated with a manufacturer? +
No. This is an independent community channel. Always verify steps against official documentation for your device.
